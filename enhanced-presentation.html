What Makes This Course "Enhanced"?

The Enhanced CHCL course builds upon the foundation of the Basic CHCL course with an additional three hours of advanced training. This enhanced certification allows you to carry in additional locations and demonstrates a higher level of proficiency.

Enhanced Benefits Include:

  • Carry at public universities and colleges (with certain restrictions)
  • Additional locations beyond basic CHCL
  • More comprehensive training in tactics and situational awareness
  • Enhanced qualification standards
  • Demonstrated commitment to responsible carry

Course Completion Requirements

  1. Complete all 8 hours of instruction (5 basic + 3 enhanced)
  2. Pass the enhanced live fire qualification
  3. Demonstrate safe firearm handling throughout
  4. Complete all required exercises and scenarios
  5. Pass written examination

Enhanced Carry Locations

Where Enhanced CHCL Allows Carry

With an Enhanced CHCL, you may carry concealed handguns in certain additional locations beyond the Basic CHCL.

Public Universities and Colleges (Act 859 of 2017)

Enhanced Carriers May Carry At:

  • Public universities and community colleges in Arkansas
  • Must be concealed at all times
  • Must have Enhanced CHCL, not just Basic
  • Subject to certain restrictions (see below)

University Carry Restrictions

Still Prohibited Even with Enhanced CHCL:
  • Athletic events (stadiums, arenas during events)
  • Disciplinary proceedings
  • Faculty/staff meetings where disciplinary matters are discussed
  • Areas where federal law prohibits (research facilities, etc.)
  • Posted buildings (if properly posted)

Other Enhanced Locations

Enhanced CHCL may also provide access to:

  • Certain state facilities (check specific regulations)
  • Additional public buildings (varies by policy)
  • Some previously restricted areas (verify current law)
Critical Reminder: Always verify current laws and policies. Property owners can still prohibit carry with proper posting. When in doubt, don't carry or contact legal counsel.

Advanced Use of Force Considerations

The Enhanced course deepens your understanding of when and how deadly force may be legally justified.

Elements of Self-Defense (Detailed Review)

1. Immediate Threat

  • Threat must be happening now (not past or future)
  • Must be more than mere words or gestures
  • Must demonstrate clear intent and ability to cause harm

2. Reasonable Belief of Death or Serious Injury

  • Would a reasonable person in your shoes believe death or serious injury was imminent?
  • Consider totality of circumstances
  • Disparity of force considerations (size, age, numbers, weapons)

3. No Duty to Retreat (Stand Your Ground)

  • Arkansas is a "stand your ground" state
  • No duty to retreat if you are in a place you have a legal right to be
  • Cannot claim self-defense if you are the initial aggressor
  • Cannot claim self-defense during commission of a felony

4. Proportional Response

  • Deadly force can only be met with deadly force
  • Cannot use deadly force to protect property alone
  • Response must be reasonable and necessary

Complex Scenarios

Scenario Analysis: In the Enhanced course, we examine multiple complex scenarios to test your understanding of when force is and is not justified. Every situation is different, and legal justification depends on specific facts.

Pre-Attack Indicators

Behavioral Indicators:

  • Target glancing (checking for witnesses/cameras)
  • Positioning to attack (closing distance, flanking)
  • Blade behavior (turning body to protect weapon side)
  • Pre-assault dialogue (interview tactics)
  • Furtive movements (reaching, adjusting clothing)

The OODA Loop

Observe → Orient → Decide → Act

  • Observe: Gather information from your environment
  • Orient: Analyze the situation based on experience and training
  • Decide: Determine the best course of action
  • Act: Execute your decision

The person who completes their OODA loop faster typically prevails in a confrontation.

Avoidance is Best

The best gunfight is the one you never get into. Use awareness to:

  • Identify potential threats early
  • Create distance and barriers
  • Choose different routes or locations
  • Call for help/backup
  • De-escalate when possible

Advanced Defensive Tactics

Enhanced training includes practical skills for various defensive scenarios.

Drawing from Concealment

Proper Draw Sequence:

  1. Clear garment: Move covering garment away from firearm
  2. Establish grip: Get full firing grip on the pistol
  3. Clear holster: Draw straight up out of holster
  4. Rotate/Index: Rotate muzzle toward target as you bring gun up
  5. Two-hand grip: Support hand meets gun
  6. Extend: Push out to target with sight picture
  7. Fire: When sights are on target and decision is made
Safety Note: Practice draw strokes with an unloaded firearm (triple check!) in a safe environment. Never draw faster than you can safely execute.

Shooting Positions

  • Isosceles: Square to target, both arms extended equally
  • Weaver: Bladed stance, push-pull arm tension
  • Modified Weaver: Hybrid position, arms approximately equal
  • Close retention: Gun held close to body in close quarters
  • One-handed shooting: Strong or weak hand only

Cover vs. Concealment

Type Definition Examples
Cover Stops bullets Brick walls, engine blocks, large trees, concrete barriers
Concealment Hides you visually only Bushes, car doors, drywall, furniture
Remember: If you can see through it, a bullet can probably go through it. Seek actual cover when possible, not just concealment.

Movement Principles

  • Move to cover, not just for movement's sake
  • A moving target is harder to hit
  • Don't move in predictable patterns
  • Maintain awareness of surroundings while moving
  • Balance speed with maintaining ability to shoot accurately

After a Defensive Shooting

Knowing what to do immediately after a defensive shooting incident is crucial.

Immediate Actions

Step 1: Ensure Safety

  • Scan for additional threats
  • Move to cover if needed
  • Don't assume the threat is neutralized
  • Keep gun ready but finger off trigger

Step 2: Call 911

  • Report that shots were fired
  • Request police and medical assistance
  • Describe yourself (so police don't mistake you for the threat)
  • Keep it brief - "I was attacked, I defended myself, please send help"

Step 3: Secure the Scene

  • Holster your weapon when it's safe to do so
  • Do not leave the scene (unless continuing danger)
  • Do not touch or move evidence
  • Point out witnesses and evidence to police

When Police Arrive

Critical Actions:
  • Follow all commands immediately
  • Keep hands visible at all times
  • Announce that you are the victim/defender
  • Announce that you are armed (if not already holstered)
  • Do not reach for your weapon - let them disarm you

Your Statement to Police

What to Say:

  • "I was in fear for my life"
  • "I was attacked and had to defend myself"
  • "I will cooperate fully after I speak with my attorney"
  • Point out evidence and witnesses
  • Sign the complaint against your attacker

What NOT to Say:

  • Don't give a detailed statement without legal counsel
  • Don't speculate about what you think happened
  • Don't say "I didn't mean to shoot" or similar
  • Don't make jokes or off-hand comments
  • Don't discuss your training or shooting experience

Get Legal Help Immediately

Even a justified shooting will result in a thorough investigation. You need legal representation. Consider:

  • Having a lawyer's contact information with you
  • Self-defense insurance (USCCA, CCW Safe, etc.)
  • Don't give detailed statements until advised by counsel
  • Remember: you have the right to remain silent - use it wisely

Enhanced Live Fire Qualification

The Enhanced CHCL requires more stringent qualification standards than the Basic course.

Enhanced Qualification Standards

Ammunition Required:

Total: 50 rounds (must be 9mm or larger caliber)

Course of Fire:

Stage Distance Rounds Time Limit Position
1 3 yards 5 10 seconds Two-handed
2 5 yards 5 10 seconds Strong hand only
3 7 yards 10 20 seconds Two-handed
4 10 yards 10 30 seconds Two-handed
5 15 yards 10 45 seconds Two-handed
6 7 yards 5 15 seconds Weak hand only
7 5 yards 5 10 seconds From holster

Passing Score

Minimum Score: 70% (35 out of 50 rounds on target)

  • All shots must hit the silhouette target
  • Shots outside the silhouette count as misses
  • Must complete all stages within time limits
  • Must demonstrate safe handling throughout
  • Any unsafe gun handling results in immediate disqualification

What to Bring to Qualification

  • Your handgun (9mm or larger caliber recommended)
  • 50 rounds of ammunition (bring extra in case of need)
  • Eye protection (ANSI rated)
  • Ear protection (electronic recommended)
  • Holster appropriate for your carry method
  • Magazine holders/spare magazines
  • Hat/cap (optional but recommended)
Safety Requirement: Any violation of the four rules of gun safety during qualification will result in immediate removal from the range and failure of the course.

Continuing Education & Training

Getting your Enhanced CHCL is just the beginning. Ongoing training is essential for maintaining skills and staying current with laws.

Practice Recommendations

Minimum Practice Schedule:

  • Dry fire practice: 10-15 minutes, 3-4 times per week
  • Live fire practice: At least once per month
  • Training courses: At least one per year
  • Review of laws: Quarterly check for updates

Skills to Maintain

  • Draw stroke from concealment
  • Rapid accurate fire at close distances
  • Precision shooting at distance
  • Shooting with one hand (strong and weak)
  • Shooting from various positions
  • Malfunction clearances
  • Emergency reloads

Advanced Training Opportunities

Consider these advanced courses to continue your education:

  • Advanced pistol courses
  • Low-light shooting
  • Force-on-force training
  • Medical training (Stop the Bleed, CPR)
  • Legal updates and seminars
  • Competition shooting (IDPA, USPSA)

Staying Current with Law Changes

Laws can and do change. Stay informed through:

  • Arkansas State Police website updates
  • Firearms law organizations (NRA, USCCA, etc.)
  • Your attorney or legal insurance provider
  • Refresher courses every 2-3 years

Course Conclusion

Congratulations!

You have completed the Arkansas Enhanced CHCL Course. This represents 8 hours of comprehensive training in concealed carry laws, tactics, and marksmanship.

Next Steps

  1. Complete Live Fire Qualification - Must score 70% or better
  2. Receive Certificate of Completion - Provided after successful qualification
  3. Submit Application to Arkansas State Police
    • Complete application form
    • Include certificate of completion
    • Pay required fees
    • Submit fingerprints
  4. Wait for Background Check - Typically 60-90 days
  5. Receive Your Enhanced CHCL - Valid for 5 years

Important Reminders

  • Your Enhanced CHCL is valid for 5 years from date of issue
  • You must carry your license with you whenever carrying concealed
  • You must present your license to law enforcement upon request
  • Notify ASP within 30 days of address change
  • Renewal requires refresher training before expiration

Final Thoughts

Carrying a concealed handgun is a serious responsibility.

You now have the legal right and the training to protect yourself and your loved ones. With this right comes the responsibility to:

  • Maintain your skills through regular practice
  • Stay current with law changes
  • Exercise good judgment in when and where to carry
  • Always prioritize avoidance and de-escalation
  • Carry responsibly and legally
