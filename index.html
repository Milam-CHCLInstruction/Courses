📋 About Our Training

We provide comprehensive, state-approved concealed handgun carry license training in Arkansas. Our courses meet all requirements set forth by Arkansas law and are taught by experienced, certified instructors.

Wes Milam is a retired police Captain with over 22 years experience. He is a certified law-enforcement instructor with over 15 years experience as a firearms instructor and a police academy instructor, covering use of force and relevant case law..

What You'll Learn

Basic CHCL Course (5 Hours):
  • Arkansas concealed carry laws and regulations
  • Safe firearm handling, storage, and transportation
  • Use of force and self-defense principles
  • Conflict avoidance and de-escalation
  • Prohibited locations and restrictions
  • Live fire qualification on the range
Enhanced CHCL Course (8 Hours Total):
  • All Basic Course content PLUS:
  • Additional 3 hours of advanced training
  • Enhanced firearm handling techniques
  • Advanced situational awareness
  • More comprehensive qualification
  • Ability to carry in additional locations (like public universities)

✅ Requirements

To Take the Course, You Must:

  • Be at least 21 years old (or 18 with military service)
  • Be a U.S. citizen or legal resident
  • Not be prohibited from possessing a firearm under state or federal law
  • Not be a fugitive from justice
  • Not have been adjudicated mentally incompetent
  • Not have been committed to a mental institution
  • Not be a habitual drunkard or unlawful user of controlled substances

What to Bring:

Required Items:
  • Valid government-issued photo ID
  • Handgun (9mm or larger caliber recommended)
  • 50 rounds of ammunition for your firearm
  • Eye and ear protection
  • Note-taking materials
  • Payment for the course
Important Note: If you don't have your own firearm, please contact us in advance. We may be able to provide a rental firearm for the live fire portion.

ℹ️ Additional Information

After Course Completion

Upon successful completion of the course, you will receive a certificate that must be submitted with your Arkansas CHCL application to the Arkansas State Police. We'll provide detailed instructions on the application process.

Class Schedule

Classes are typically held on weekends and select weekdays. Contact us for the next available class date and to reserve your spot. Class sizes are limited to ensure quality instruction.

Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance. Your payment can be transferred to a future class date.

Note: This course satisfies the training requirements for an Arkansas Concealed Handgun Carry License as mandated by Arkansas Code Annotated § 5-73-309. The final license is issued by the Arkansas State Police, not by our training facility.
